# Beautiful Minds Child Care Website - Design Guide

## 🎨 Design Inspiration: Hometown Preschool Layout

This website is designed to match the layout and feel of Hometown Preschool while featuring Beautiful Minds Child Care's unique branding and information.

## 📐 Website Sections

### 1. **Navigation Header**
- Clean white background with Beautiful Minds logo
- Navigation menu with: Home, Programs, About, Our Team, Contact
- Mobile hamburger menu for smaller screens

### 2. **Hero Section with Wavy Design**
- Wavy decorative elements top and bottom
- Left side: Space for hero image (children at play)
- Right side: Gradient box with key messaging
  - Headline: "A Place for All Children to Feel Included"
  - Tagline: "Serving Eau Claire families since 2012"
  - Description: "Every child is seen, loved, celebrated, and valued."
  - CTA: Schedule a Tour button

### 3. **Classroom Stages Section**
- Shows different age groups with icons
- **Tiny Tigers, Baby Bears, Peguins, Turtles** (4 weeks - 2 years)
- **Ponies, Monkeys, Frogs** (2 - 3 years)
- **Parrots** (4K Program)
- **Zoo** (School Age 5 - 12 years)

### 4. **More Than Childcare - It's Family Section**
- Teal background with purple border
- Features 4 core values:
  - Relationships First
  - Every Child is Unique
  - Partners with Families
  - Joyful Learning
- Award/badge area

### 5. **Dual Call-to-Action**
- "Ready to See It for Yourself?" - Schedule a Tour
- "Want to Join Our Team?" - Apply Today

### 6. **Our Team Section**
- Tonya Bandoli - Director & Owner
- Ashley Matysik - Assistant Director
- Lead Educators - Infant & Toddler
- Lead Educators - Preschool & Pre-K
- Support Staff

### 7. **Contact Section**
- Contact form
- Full center information
- Social media links
- Hours and location

### 8. **Footer**
- Dark background
- Logo and mission statement
- Quick links
- Contact information

## 📝 Easy Editing Tips

### Update Hero Image
1. Find a nice photo of children at Beautiful Minds
2. Save as `assets/hero-image.jpg`
3. The image will automatically display in the hero section

### Change Classroom Stage Names
Open `index.html` and find:
```html
<div class="stage-card">
    <h3>Tiny Tigers</h3>
    <p>4 weeks - 2 years</p>
</div>
```

### Customize Values
Find the "More Than Childcare" section and edit the 4 values to match your center.

### Add Staff Members
Edit the staff cards with actual staff names and information.

### Update Contact Info
Replace:
- 2821 Fairfax Street, Eau Claire, WI 54701
- (715) 834-4360
- admin@bmindscc.com

## 🖼️ Hero Image Recommendations

The hero image area should show:
- Children engaged in activities
- A warm, welcoming classroom environment
- Real Beautiful Minds moments
- Diversity and inclusion

**Image size:** Square or near-square ratio works best
